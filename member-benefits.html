— Questions

Frequently asked.

Is membership really free?

Yes — completely free, with no hidden fees, no trials, and no expiry date. You sign up once and keep your benefits forever.

How is the 15% discount applied?

The member rate appears automatically once you're signed in. No promo codes needed — every listing shows your discounted price by default.

Can I cancel a booking for free?

Members can cancel at no cost right up to the day of arrival. If you cancel on the arrival date itself, a one-night charge applies.

Does the 7+ night discount stack with the member rate?

It does. For stays of seven nights or more, the 25% long-stay discount is applied on top of your 15% member rate — so you're saving even more.

Is early check-in guaranteed?

Early check-in from 2 PM is available subject to same-day availability. In most cases it's confirmed — we'll let you know by noon on the day.

What's included in the welcome package?

A selection of drinks and snacks sourced from local producers — waiting at the door when you arrive. The selection changes by season and location.
